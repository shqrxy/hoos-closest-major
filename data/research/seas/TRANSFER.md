# SEAS internal transfer and first-year curriculum

Researched 2026-11-09. The Undergraduate Record (records.ureg.virginia.edu) could not be read, so this file uses engineering.virginia.edu and uvacsadvising.org pages.

## Internal (intra-university) transfer into Engineering

Source: https://engineering.virginia.edu/current-students/current-undergraduate-students/transferring-uva-engineering

### Eligibility
- Current UVA student in good standing, with a **cumulative GPA above 2.0**.
- Not on academic probation or warning in the home school when applying.
- A "demonstrated history of success and progress toward an engineering degree" (a technical record). The page says students without one "will not be accepted for transfer."
- The page states **no minimum grade** for individual core courses.

### Required core courses (all finished before applying)
| Requirement | Accepted courses |
|---|---|
| Calculus I | APMA 1090 or MATH 1310 (required for students starting Fall 2025 or later) |
| Calculus II | APMA 1110 or MATH 1320 |
| Calculus III | APMA 2120 or MATH 2310 |
| Chemistry I lecture | CHEM 1410, CHEM 1610, or CHEM 1810 (fall only) |
| Chemistry I lab | CHEM 1411, CHEM 1611, or CHEM 1811 (fall only) |
| Introduction to programming | CS 1110, CS 1111, CS 1112, or CS 1113 |
| Physics I lecture | PHYS 1420, PHYS 1425, or PHYS 1710 |
| Physics I lab | PHYS 1429 or PHYS 2030 |

### Timing
- Application windows:
  - **Nov 15 to Dec 15** for spring admission. Decisions come about Jan 5.
  - **May 1 to Jun 1** for fall admission. Decisions come about Jul 1.
- Applications are evaluated after semester grades are posted. No late applications are accepted.
- A May–June applicant with one or two prerequisites still in progress over the summer can note this in the application. The application then stays "pending" until those courses are done.
- The applicant names an intended major. "All students accepted for transfer are placed into their major of choice."
- A 3rd- or 4th-year applicant needs the desired major to support the transfer, so the student makes satisfactory progress without adding time to the degree.
- Contact: engineering-undgrad@virginia.edu.

### Related rules
- No double major combining Engineering and McIntire (source: SEAS Majors and Minors page).
- CS-specific rule: current SEAS students may switch to or add the B.S. in Computer Science only after earning a **C- or better in CS 2100** (source: https://uvacsadvising.org/bscs.html). After transferring into SEAS, students declare on arrival.

## Standard SEAS first-year curriculum

Sources:
- 2026 New Student Orientation deck: https://engineering.virginia.edu/sites/default/files/_common/offices/UndergradPrograms/Summer%202026%20New%20Student%20Orientation%20(Updated%20Compressed).pdf
- Summer Advising page: https://engineering.virginia.edu/undergraduate-study/current-undergrads/new-students/summer-advising-and-enrollment
- Engineering Foundations: https://engineering.virginia.edu/offices-programs/first-year-engineering/engineering-foundations-program
- BSCS example schedule: https://uvacsadvising.org/materials/bscs-example-schedule.pdf

The Summer Advising page lists the core first-year courses. "By the end of spring semester, you should have credit for the core curriculum."
- Calculus I and II: APMA 1090 (3) and APMA 1110 (4). Students start at APMA 1090, 1110, or 2120 depending on the APMA placement test.
- CHEM 1410 (3) and CHEM 1411 (1), General Chemistry and lab
- ENGR 1010 (4), Engineering Foundations 1, in the fall. Rodman Scholars take ENGR 1410.
- ENGR 1020 (3), Engineering Foundations 2, in the spring. Rodman Scholars take ENGR 1420.
- CS 1110, CS 1111, CS 1112, or CS 1113 (3), Intro to Programming. CS 1112 is only for students with no programming experience.
- PHYS 1425 (3) and PHYS 1429 (1), Physics I and lab
- Math and Science Elective I (3). Options: BIOL 2100, BIOL 2200, CE 2001, CHEM 1420, PHYS 2620, MSE 2090, EVSC 2800, EVSC 3200, EVSC 3300, or any non-required APMA course at 2000 or above.
- One Humanities and Social Science elective (3)

**Standard fall semester** (2026 orientation deck):
- APMA 1090, 1110, or 2120 (per placement)
- CHEM 1410 and CHEM 1411
- ENGR 1010
- HSS elective

**Spring semester:** The orientation deck's table could not be extracted. Based on the core list and the BSCS example, spring is typically:
- Next APMA course
- ENGR 1020
- CS 111X
- PHYS 1425 and PHYS 1429
- Math/Science elective or HSS

Example from the BSCS plan, for a student who placed into APMA 1110:
- Fall: APMA 1110, CHEM 1410/1411, ENGR 1010, HSS/UE (15 credits)
- Spring: CS 111X, APMA 2120, ENGR 1020, PHYS 1425/1429, Math/Sci elective (17 credits)

Placement and credit notes:
- Students with credit for a first-year course swap in another one, such as:
  - Higher math for APMA 2120 credit
  - CS, a Math/Sci elective, or Physics for CHEM 1410 credit
- Placing out of a course gives no credit.
- CS place-out tests are held in the first week of classes.

### Declaring a major
- First-years are "undeclared" and move into a major at the end of the first year.
- The Major Declaration deadline is **March 1** of the first-year spring (2026 orientation deck).
- "Undeclared" is not a degree program.

### SEAS-wide general requirements (all B.S. degrees, from the BSCS page and the E&S curriculum page)
- APMA 1090, 1110, and 2120 (most majors also need more APMA)
- CHEM 1410/1411
- PHYS 1425/1429 and PHYS 2415/2419 (some majors may use ECE 2200)
- ENGR 1010 and 1020
- CS 111X
- **STS 2600** Engineering Ethics. It is now the required lower-level STS course. The notes' "STS 1500" is out of date.
- **STS 4500** in fall of 4th year and **STS 4600** in spring of 4th year (the thesis sequence)
- Math/Science elective (3)
- HSS electives (at least 9). Required STS courses don't count as HSS. Other STS courses count, except STS 1800 and STS 4810.
- Unrestricted electives. APMA 1090 and COMM courses count as UE.
